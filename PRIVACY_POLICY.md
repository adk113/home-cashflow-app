# Privacy Policy for Home CashFlow Tracker

*Last Updated: October 14, 2026*

**Home CashFlow Tracker** ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates and handles user information.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Storage (Local-First Architecture)

- **Zero Remote Data Collection**: Home CashFlow Tracker is an entirely **offline, local-first** financial management tool. 
- **On-Device Storage**: All transaction entries, income records, expenses, categories, notes, and currency preferences are stored **strictly on your local device** using an encrypted local SQLite database and secure on-device storage.
- **No Cloud Synchronization**: We do not maintain external servers, databases, or accounts that receive, store, or process your personal financial data.

---

## 2. Permissions

The App requests **no invasive system permissions**:
- No location access
- No camera access
- No contacts or phone state access
- No audio or microphone access
- No background tracking

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do not embed third-party tracking SDKs (such as Facebook SDK, Google Analytics for Firebase, or advertising trackers).
- **No Advertising**: The App is free of third-party advertisements and does not share device identifiers with ad networks.

---

## 4. Data Safety (Google Play Console Compliance)

When answering Google Play's Data Safety questionnaire:
- **Data Collected**: No user data collected.
- **Data Shared**: No user data shared with third parties.
- **Security Practices**: All data remains protected within your operating system's application sandbox on your device.
